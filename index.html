Frequently asked questions

Short answers about how DigiSolution works with businesses.

What does DigiSolution do?

We build websites, mobile apps, databases and custom business systems, and add AI integrations and automations that remove repetitive manual work.

What is the automation audit?

The audit is a first conversation about your business. We look at how work moves through your tools today and identify the best first thing to automate or build, so you know what we would do first and why.

Do I need to know what to build before I get in touch?

No. Many projects start with a problem such as too many spreadsheets or too much copy-pasting between tools. In the Discover stage we work out the right solution with you and agree what to build.

How do you price projects?

Every project is scoped individually. We agree what to build during Discover, so the scope is clear before development starts.

Can you add AI to the tools we already use?

Yes. AI integrations are added to your existing tools and workflows rather than replacing them, so your team keeps working in a familiar way wherever that makes sense.

Do you support the product after launch?

Yes. After launch we hand over and stay available to improve and extend what we built.
